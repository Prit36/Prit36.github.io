Step into the painting

The bay from the banner on my page, as a place you can walk around. It keeps Surat's hours, the way the banner does: right now it is some hour.

Eleven paper lanterns are hidden around the bay, one for each project I've built this year. Find them and light them.

Left thumb to walk, right thumb to look. Tap what you want to use.
